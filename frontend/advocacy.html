Personal Mission

Everyone has their own lived experiences with chronic illness. Whether you are navigating those challenges yourself or grieving loved ones affected by them, I believe storytelling can become a powerful form of healing and closure of one's experience.

"Hi, I'm Chelsey a STEM enthusiast and advocate from Maui, Hawaiʻi. I am passionate about empowering youth to recognize the value and strength of their voices."

My hope is that this platform fosters a compassionate community where people can share their experiences openly, find comfort in one another's stories, and remind each other that no one has to go through illness alone.
