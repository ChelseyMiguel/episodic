Our Story

A sanctuary for the resilient, the poetic, and the chronically bold.

Episodic was founded by Chelsey Miguel, a STEM enthusiast and advocate from Maui, Hawaiʻi, after she was diagnosed with lupus during high school. "I felt like one of the best ways to cope with my diagnosis was the form of art and storytelling. I want to harbor a safe space where people can freely express themselves throughout the emotional turmoil or about their own lived experience."

We believe illness doesn't pause a life. It creates a different kind of life. Episodic is the mixture of poetry, personal essay, and advocacy.
