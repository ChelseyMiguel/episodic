Poem of the Week

The Waiting Room

Chelsey Miguel

On the bleakness of waiting, the loneliness of patience, and the quiet wish to not face it alone.

For other people patience is not a given
Patience in my eyes is the bleakness of these never-ending walls
It's the wonder of lying awake at night with no emotion
The uncertainty ringing amongst telephone calls

For others, time is persisting
For me, I am still waiting–
Tapping my shoe, biting the blues

Time is a tightening noose
But patience is lonely
Can you wait with me too?
